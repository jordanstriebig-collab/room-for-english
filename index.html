Before you plan around this
  • Humanities Analytics minor (2026–27 catalog and English department site): 76-275, 76-380, two core courses, one List A and one List B elective. List A and List B combine the catalog’s lists with the department site’s; 36-200 counts in place of 36-201 (its credit equivalent). List A courses are technical, so they go in free electives, not humanities gen-ed slots. 76-388 is left out of the core options because students who have taken 15-110 or 15-112 can’t take it. A List A course the major already requires (for example 15-110 or 36-202) counts as one of the minor’s two double-counted courses. For Information Systems majors the minor can replace the IS concentration, so it may use the concentration slots.
  • Free electives can hold any English course. Gen-ed slots are checked against approved course lists for SCS majors (2026–27 catalog Humanities & Arts rules), Dietrich majors (Dietrich Gen Ed course search, Fall 2026 and Spring 2027), MCS majors (MCS nontechnical breadth lists), Tepper majors (University Core rules) and engineering majors (College of Engineering Gen Ed course search, Spring and Fall 2026 lists): an English course fills a gen-ed slot only if it’s approved for that category, and each approved course counts once. Other colleges’ gen-ed slots still assume any English course can count, until their approved lists are added. A course that fills a student’s home-college gen-ed can also count toward an English minor or major; this is standard across CMU (confirmed with the English department, 10/29). Turn off “Count gen-ed slots” to see the strict case.
  • English courses are placed one per open slot, in prerequisite order from the 2026–27 catalog (for example 76-271 before 76-391 before 76-487, 76-239 before 76-259 and 76-310, an intro genre course before a workshop) and only in a term they run. Requirements that name a level rather than a course (such as “300/400-level”) need first-year writing first, and workshops need an intro genre course first. Grade minimums (such as a B in the intro course) aren’t checked.
  • For English additional majors, up to two courses can double-count with a primary major outside English (Department of English policy), or fewer if the primary major’s own rule is stricter. The tool uses this for track electives in Technical Writing and the production elective in Film & Visual Media when a matching course is already in the STEM plan, and marks them “also counts for your major”. Each double-counted course must be approved by an advisor. Gen-ed and free-elective slots are not treated as double-counting.
  • Additional majors in Technical Writing assume the calculus, statistics and programming prerequisites are already covered by your STEM major. Their core is the six courses the 2026–27 catalog lists (42 units), plus three theory/specialization courses and three track electives.
  • Every plan starts from no AP or transfer credit, so calculus (21-120) and intro programming (15-110 or 15-112) are included. Use “Credit you already have” to remove courses you have credit for; later courses then move earlier where their prerequisites and fall/spring offerings allow, and the freed units become open slots. Credit counts toward the 360 units but check with your advisor how CMU applies your specific scores.
  • Course order follows the 2026–27 catalog's prerequisites, and each course is placed only in a term it runs on the Pittsburgh campus: fall if it's on the Fall 2026 schedule of classes, spring if it's on the Spring 2027 schedule. A course on neither schedule (not offered this year) follows the catalog's term. Corequisites from the catalog's course descriptions are kept together: each one is taken the same semester or earlier (for example 21-120 with 33-121 or 33-141, 09-231 with 09-344), and 76-487 and its lab 76-488 always share a semester.
  • Most plans follow a department sample schedule. Where the catalog has none (several Dietrich and MCS majors, and the Tepper Economics majors), the plan was built from the catalog requirements in prerequisite order, with gen ed slots shown by category. The note under each plan says which. For the Tepper majors, free electives include the required minor's units.
  • In “Already at CMU”, a course you add with the search box fills the most specific requirement it qualifies for (a major elective list, a gen-ed category, a Humanities & Arts elective), and only then a free elective. Major elective lists come from the 2026–27 catalog; where the catalog gives a rule instead of a list (for example “16-3xx or 16-4xx”), the course number is used. Dietrich gen-ed matches use the Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 approved lists; a course approved only in other semesters may not be recognized, so check with your advisor.
  • Talk to your advisor and the English department before declaring. This is a planning estimate, not a degree audit.
